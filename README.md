I graduated in **Robotics and Artificial Intelligence** from **Hanoi University of Industry**, and now work as a Robotics Software Engineer at **VinRobotics**. 

With two years of shipping ROS 2 mobile robots into production — hospitals, restaurants, factory floors — and researching Embodied AI along the way, I focus on building robust systems. I recently published in **MethodsX (Q2)** on distributional reinforcement learning for path planning under sensor noise.

<br/>

### <img width="35" height="35" alt="robot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2c1c8321-0720-4372-9780-b29390485593" /> What I actually do

Most robotics demos work in ideal conditions — my job is making them work when they don't: partial LiDAR scans, reflective floors, dropped network packets, and operators expecting the robot to keep moving anyway.

- **Autonomous Navigation:** ROS 2, Nav2, Cartographer SLAM, DDS QoS tuning for real-time performance on constrained hardware.
- **Embodied AI & RL:** Manipulation policy training in MuJoCo, sim-to-real transfer, distributional RL (QR-DQN / C51) for risk-aware path planning.
- **3D Perception:** Point cloud and depth-image pipelines (Intel D435, Visionary-T) for 3D object detection in unstructured environments.
- **Embedded Robotics:** Micro-ROS firmware on ESP32 with FreeRTOS — sub-5ms sensor publish latency for tight control loops.
