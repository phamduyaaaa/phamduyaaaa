I graduated in **Robotics and Artificial Intelligence** from **Hanoi University of Industry**, and now work as a **Robotics Software Engineer at VinRobotics** and **Lab Leader at HalBotLab**. Two years shipping ROS 2 mobile robots into production — hospitals, restaurants, factory floors — and researching Embodied AI along the way. Published in *MethodsX* (Q2) on distributional reinforcement learning for path planning under sensor noise.

<br/>

## <img width="40" height="40" alt="robot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2c1c8321-0720-4372-9780-b29390485593" /> What I actually do

Most robotics demos work in ideal conditions — my job is making them work when they don't: partial LiDAR scans, reflective floors, dropped network packets, operators expecting the robot to keep moving anyway.

- 🤖 **Autonomous Navigation**: ROS 2, Nav2, Cartographer SLAM, DDS QoS tuning for real-time performance on constrained hardware.
- 🧠 **Embodied AI & Reinforcement Learning**: manipulation policy training in MuJoCo, sim-to-real transfer, distributional RL (QR-DQN / C51) for risk-aware path planning.
- 👁️ **3D Perception**: point cloud and depth-image pipelines (Intel D435, Visionary-T) for 3D object detection in unstructured environments.
- ⚙️ **Embedded Robotics**: Micro-ROS firmware on ESP32 with FreeRTOS — sub-5ms sensor publish latency for tight control loops.

<br/>

## 💼 Experience

**VinRobotics** — Robotics Software Engineer · *Mar 2026 – Present*
Designed a robot license management system (cryptographic activation, feature gating, telemetry) for fleet deployments; deployed ROS 2 modules for factory floor QC automation.

**ByteHome Robotics** — Robotics Software Engineer, Tech Lead · *Oct 2025 – Mar 2026*
Led a 5-engineer team building differential-drive service robots for hospitality/healthcare (full ROS 2 stack: SLAM, Nav2, multilingual voice interaction). Took the product from prototype to commercial deployment and trained 30+ B2B clients to operate it independently.

**HalBotLab** (Hanoi Intelligence Robot Laboratory) — Lab Leader · *Jun 2024 – Present*
Directing research on Embodied AI: representation learning from egocentric observations, point cloud/depth processing pipelines, and Micro-ROS/FreeRTOS firmware.
